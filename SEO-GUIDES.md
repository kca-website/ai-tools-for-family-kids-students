# SEO entry pages

These pages are intentionally isolated from the main app so they can attract Greek-language organic search without adding visual clutter to the homepage or touching quiz/Learning Path logic.

Pages:
- /ai-ergaleia-gia-mathites.html
- /ai-gia-dimotiko.html
- /ai-gia-gymnasio.html
- /asfales-ai-gia-paidia.html

Discovery is handled through /seo-sitemap.xml, referenced from robots.txt. The pages cross-link to the existing app, diagnostics, age-specific routes, accessibility, and Greek Sign Language resources.
