## English — social post
New on AI Tools 4 Kids: **Creation Missions**.

What if you had to improve your school canteen, organise a school festival, design an accessible app, or manage a small technical project?

Across 8 interactive missions, students from primary school to Lyceum and vocational education make decisions, manage imaginary budgets, and discover what worked, what failed, and why.

Older students also explore the fundamentals of project management: goals, deliverables, timelines, budgets, and risk management.

At the end, they present their own solution and can optionally request short, constructive AI feedback on their writing. The aim is not grading, but learning through reflection and improvement.

Free, bilingual, with no account required.
