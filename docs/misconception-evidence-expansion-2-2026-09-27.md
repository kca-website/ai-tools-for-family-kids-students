# Misconception Evidence Expansion 2 — 2026-09-27

This batch adds six records to the existing evidence layer, increasing coverage from 18 to 24 research-backed gaps.

## Added

- Ratio/proportion reasoning
- First-law thermodynamics energy balance
- Cell-membrane transport/function
- Specific vs non-specific immune reasoning
- Producers and energy sources in ecosystems
- DNA as genetic material

## Deliberately not added

The audit reviewed additional verified curriculum gaps but did not enable them where the available source was too broad or did not match the exact misconception statement closely enough. Examples include:

- GCD vs LCM confusion
- “every quadratic has two real roots”
- `sqrt(x²)=x` without absolute value
- heterozygous vs homozygous genotype

These remain eligible for future evidence review but are not surfaced in misconception-aware teacher assessment yet.

## Integrity

The existing integrity test remains the gatekeeper: every research record must map to an existing quiz gap, a verified curriculum section and a stable source record.
