Key finding: Greek Sign Language (GSL)

In the sources checked for the 43 tools, no documented support for Greek Sign Language (GSL) was found.

The wording is deliberately careful: this is an absence of documentation that we could find, not proof that no tool has or could acquire such a capability. Nor does it mean that a tool without GSL is inaccessible overall for deaf or hard-of-hearing students; it may offer subtitles, transcription, visual alternatives or other accessibility features.

The initial research was carried out on 27 August 2026 and the inventory was extended on 22 September 2026. If newer official documentation is found, the record should be updated.

Accessibility statement of aitools4kids.gr

Our goal is for the core experience of aitools4kids.gr to be usable by as many students, parents and educators as possible, including users with disabilities.

  • On 7 September 2026 a technical check was carried out on representative states of the homepage, the Primary zone, AI Help and this page, using free open-source tools (axe-core and Playwright) and the WCAG 2.0/2.1 A–AA and WCAG 2.2 AA rules supported by axe-core.
  • After the fixes, the automated re-check found no violations in the specific states checked. We also checked keyboard navigation in the AI Help information dialog, closing with Escape, focus return and the basic robustness of the page without JavaScript.
  • This check does not cover every page, every possible state of use or full testing with all screen readers and assistive technologies. For that reason we do not present the site as fully certified or as proven to conform to WCAG 2.2 AA.
  • Accessibility is treated as an ongoing process. New features and changes will continue to be checked before production release wherever practically possible.

Statement last updated: 7 September 2026.
