AI Transparency

Ελληνικά

When you use AI Help, the Parent Assistant or the Educator AI Assistant, you are interacting with an artificial intelligence system, not a human.

Active server-side configuration

Checking the active model…

Production default: Cloudflare Workers AI · @cf/openai/gpt-oss-120b, with Groq as the first fallback and Google Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite as the second fallback, where configured. Puter is an optional alternative and is loaded only when the user chooses it.

What is sent

In the server-side AI Help, your message is sent together with the necessary educational context. If you upload a PDF, only the extracted text needed for the answer is sent. You should not enter personal or sensitive student data.

Quality feedback

The 👍/👎 buttons record only an anonymous quality event and limited technical context. The text of the question or the answer is not sent.

Model policy

Production GPT-OSS 120B remains the main server-side model. With the Gemini fallback the model changes, but the same rules for educational help and verification of school sources still apply. Newer preview models, such as Qwen 3.8 27B, are evaluated first and do not automatically become the default.

AI Act

The transparency obligations of Article 50 apply from 2 August 2026. For interactive AI, the core principle is that users know they are interacting with AI. The rules for certain high-risk uses in education apply from 2 December 2027. aitools4kids.gr does not use the tutor for official grading, admission, ranking or diagnosis of students.
