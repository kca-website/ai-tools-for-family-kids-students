Do you want to create something with AI?

Images, posters, timelines, short comics and visual projects can use AI without turning it into a teacher. The idea, choices and final result should remain yours.

  • Google Arts & Culture: visual exploration of history, culture and collections.

A useful creative rule

First decide what you want to say. Then choose a tool to make it clearer, more visual or more engaging. Starting with “make something for me” often produces attractive work that is not really yours.

Ideas that are not ready-made assignments

  • A timeline using six facts you already verified.
  • An infographic built from your own notes or data.
  • A four-panel comic explaining a concept in your own words.
  • A poster with three key messages and real sources.
  • A visual idea map before you start writing.
