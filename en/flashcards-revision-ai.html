Revision

Do you want flashcards, short quizzes or spaced repetition?

Revision tools work best after you have already engaged with the material. They are useful for checking what you remember, not for replacing the first step of understanding.

Quick choice:
  • Quizlet: an established platform for cards and different study modes.
  • Knowt: a newer option with free study modes and card creation from files.
  • AI Help: when you want one guided question at a time instead of a large card set.

Quizlet — organised cards and practice

Quizlet is practical for vocabulary, dates, key concepts and short question-answer pairs. Its terms say the service is primarily intended for users over 13, with a restricted experience for younger users.

Knowt — another free study-mode option

Knowt offers flashcards, Learn mode, practice tests, matching and spaced repetition on its free tier, with limits on some AI usage. It can also generate cards from PDFs and notes.

For minors

The service requires users to be 13+ and states that verifiable parent or guardian consent is needed before AI functionality is used by someone under 18.

Make better cards

  • Keep one idea per card instead of copying a full paragraph.
  • Include “why?” and “how?” questions, not only definitions.
  • If AI generates the cards, check them against your book or notes before studying.
  • Try to answer before revealing the solution.
Useful prompt

“Turn these notes into ten short questions. Make four of them understanding questions, not simple recall. Do not show the answer until I reply.”
