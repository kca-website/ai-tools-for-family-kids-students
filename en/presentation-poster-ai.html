Do you want to make a presentation, poster or infographic?

AI is most useful here as a design assistant: layout ideas, images, headings and visual structure. You should still understand and verify the content of the schoolwork yourself.

Quick choice:
  • Canva: for presentations, posters, timelines and infographics.
  • Adobe Express for Education: a K-12 option through an educational environment.
  • AutoDraw: simple visual creation without a general chatbot.

Canva — fast visual organisation

Canva is useful when you already have the content and want to turn it into a presentation, poster or infographic. Canva’s AI products are generally for users aged 13+ unless they are used through Canva Education under educational supervision.

See the Canva card →

Adobe Express for Education — a school-focused option

Adobe Express for Education is a free creative application for K-12 students and supports presentations, posters, animations and webpages. The education version includes school-oriented safety and generative-AI guardrails.

Access

Children under 13 use it through a school or educational deployment. A personal Adobe ID has a minimum age of 13.

AutoDraw — when a simple tool is enough

Not every creative task needs generative AI. AutoDraw helps turn a rough sketch into a cleaner icon and can be useful for posters, idea maps and small projects.

See the AutoDraw card →

A better workflow

Write the three to five points you want to communicate first. Then use the design tool for layout and visual presentation. That keeps the work yours.

Be careful with AI images

  • Do not present an AI-generated image as a real historical or scientific photograph.
  • Use real sources for factual claims.
  • Do not upload photos of children or other people without a clear reason and permission.
