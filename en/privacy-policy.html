Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026 · Ελληνικά

Short answer: The guide, the Practice Map, the learning paths and the tool recommendations work without an account on aitools4kids.gr. Features that generate an answer with AI are optional and are activated only when the user chooses them. If you use server-side AI, the text you submit may be sent first to Cloudflare Workers AI and, if that is unavailable or has reached a usage limit, to Groq and then to Google Gemini as fallback providers. Puter remains an optional alternative when the user selects it. If you upload a PDF, Word file, text file or photo in a supported feature, the file is read locally in your browser and only the extracted text needed for the answer is sent to the AI provider. In AI Study, the subject and unit you choose are sent to our server so that the relevant excerpt of the official school textbook can be retrieved.

1. Who we are

AI Tools for Kids (aitools4kids.gr) is an independent educational project for parents, students and educators. It is not an official product or partnership of any AI provider.

2. The core platform

For the guide, the tools, the Practice Map, the prompts, the learning paths and the information pages:

  • We do not ask for a name or email to use the core platform.
  • We do not create a student profile or a personal performance history in our own database.
  • We do not use advertising cookies and we do not track users across websites.
  • Your browser may store non-sensitive preferences locally, such as language or selected zone, as well as local progress/revision data from the Practice Map, so that you can continue on the same device. This data stays in the browser's localStorage and is not stored in our own database.
  • For faster loading and basic offline behaviour, the PWA service worker may keep static site files in Cache Storage. /api/ routes are excluded and API responses are not stored in the PWA cache.

3. AI features via Cloudflare Workers AI, Groq and Google Gemini

The Educator AI Assistant, the optional AI Help/Parent Assistant and some creative educational features use server-side AI routing. The default production order is Cloudflare Workers AI with GPT-OSS 120B as the primary provider and Groq with GPT-OSS 120B as the fallback. If neither provider is available, Google Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite is used through the server, where configured. The active configuration is published on the AI Transparency page.

  • Visitors do not need a Cloudflare, Groq or Google account.
  • The API key is never sent to the browser; it is used only by the server.
  • The text the user submits, together with the necessary educational context of the selected class, subject and unit, is sent to the active server-side provider to generate the answer. If the primary provider fails because of a limit, temporary unavailability or a technical error, the same necessary request may be sent to the fallback provider.
  • aitools4kids.gr does not store the generated material or the prompt in its own database.
  • Do not enter student names, contact details, diagnoses, health data or other personal/sensitive data.

Data processing by third parties is governed by the respective terms and privacy policies of Cloudflare, Groq and Google. aitools4kids.gr does not use the content of prompts for advertising profiling and does not store prompts or answers in its own database. For organised use in secondary education, see also the AI use at school page.

4. Optional use of Puter

In some features of the site, including the alternative option in the Educator AI Assistant and in AI Help/Parent Assistant, Puter may be used.

  • Puter is loaded only when the user chooses it.
  • You may be asked to sign in to a Puter account.
  • The submitted text is passed through Puter to the relevant AI provider.
  • aitools4kids.gr does not store Puter conversations in its own database.

5. PDFs and study files

In supported features, such as AI Help, AI Study (the PDF/notes study route) and the assistants in “What do you want to do with AI?” (e.g. research, flashcards, presentations, reading, creative work, study planning), the user can optionally select a PDF, Word (.docx), plain text (.txt/.md) file or a photo.

  • Reading and extracting text from the file happens locally in the browser.
  • To read PDF and Word files, the browser loads open-source libraries (pdf.js, mammoth) from the public jsDelivr CDN. Your file is not sent there.
  • The file itself is not stored in any aitools4kids.gr database.
  • Only the extracted text used as context is sent to the selected AI provider for the answer.
  • Do not upload documents containing names, contact details, health data, school identifiers or other personal/sensitive data.
  • In AI Study and the “What do you want to do with AI?” assistants you can also upload a photo or a scanned PDF (up to 5 pages). Text recognition (OCR) happens locally in the browser with the open-source Tesseract.js library, whose files are loaded from jsDelivr. The image is not sent to us or to the AI provider; only the recognised text is sent.
  • A photo may contain more than you notice at first glance (e.g. names in a notebook). Make sure no personal details are visible.

6. AI Study and the official school textbook

AI Study works without an account. For selected subjects it is based on the official school textbook (ebooks.edu.gr).

  • When you choose a subject and unit, the subject and unit title are sent to the aitools4kids.gr server so that the relevant excerpt of the official school textbook can be retrieved from ebooks.edu.gr. No name, email or other personal identifier is sent in this request.
  • The textbook excerpt, your study choices (e.g. goal and time) and any text or notes you provide are sent to the server-side AI provider described in section 3 to produce the summary, the questions and the study plan.
  • If there is no mapped official source for a selection, the feature says so and does not present content as the official school textbook.
  • Your study choices (e.g. level, subject, unit, study mode) and, if you uploaded a PDF, only the file name and page count are kept temporarily in your browser's sessionStorage so they can carry over between pages. They are deleted when you close the tab and are not stored in our own database.
  • Your browser may also store locally, in localStorage, the language you chose and non-sensitive progress/revision data from practice features, so you can continue from the same point on the same device. This data stays on your device and is not stored in our own database.

7. External links

The platform contains links to external tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, DeepSeek, Perplexity, MagicSchool and Brisk. When you open such a link, you leave aitools4kids.gr and the terms and privacy policy of that provider apply.

To display some interface elements, the browser may make direct requests to Google services: Google Fonts for typefaces and the Google S2 Favicons service for small icons of external tools. In these requests the third-party provider receives the usual technical data of a web connection (such as IP address and browser details). aitools4kids.gr does not use these requests for advertising profiling.

On the Greek Sign Language page, some reference images may be loaded from Wikimedia Commons. In that case the browser connects directly to Wikimedia to fetch the image.

8. Vercel Analytics

We use Vercel Analytics for aggregate measurement of traffic and technical use of the platform. We do not use it for advertising profiling or for tracking users on other websites.

9. Homepage search

The homepage search runs locally in the browser on aitools4kids.gr data. The text you type in the search box is not sent to an AI provider and is not recorded as a search query in Vercel Analytics.

10. Anonymous feedback in AI Help

The 👍/👎 buttons use Vercel Analytics for aggregate quality measurement. We do not send the text of the question or the answer, a name, email or any other identifier. Only the feedback choice and limited technical context are recorded.

11. AI transparency

AI screens state that the user is interacting with an artificial intelligence system. The active server-side model configuration is shown on the AI Transparency page.

12. Ages and supervision

The direct student AI Help of aitools4kids.gr is available only in the upper-secondary (Lykeio) zone. The Parent Assistant is available at all three school levels. Students should use external services only when they meet the age requirements of the relevant provider and, where required, with parental consent or supervision. The Educator AI Assistant is intended for adult educators.
