Research

Do you want to research a topic and know where each claim comes from?

For school research, the key question is not whether AI writes smoothly. It is whether it helps you locate sources, open them and verify that they actually support the claim.

Quick choice:
  • Perplexity: for initial web research with visible source links.
  • NotebookLM: when you already have a defined set of sources.
  • Search and primary sources: for the final verification step.

Perplexity — start from sources, not a finished essay

Perplexity is useful when exploring a topic and you want links to the material used in its answer. A citation is not proof by itself: open the source and check whether it actually supports the specific claim.

Age rule

The service’s current terms require users to be at least 13. A parent or guardian must accept the terms on behalf of a minor aged 13+ before use.

Useful research prompt

“Find four reliable sources on this topic. For each one, tell me who published it, what it supports and which point I should verify myself.”

The three-check rule

  • Who? Who published the source and why are they credible on this topic?
  • When? Is it recent enough for the question you are answering?
  • Does it really say that? Open the source and locate the supporting passage or evidence.

Do not submit the AI answer as your assignment

Use the tool to find and organise material. Then read the sources you keep and write the final work in your own words.
