AI use at school

A short, practical overview of the organised use of Artificial Intelligence systems in Greek secondary education. · Ελληνικά

Legal basis: Joint Ministerial Decision (KYA) 55909/Δ6/2026, Government Gazette B΄ 2639/12.05.2026, “Determination of matters relating to the use of artificial intelligence systems in the educational process and the protection of personal data”.

What it provides, in short

  • Scope: the decision applies to secondary schools and to programmes or activities carried out as part of the curriculum, at school or in distance education. Article 2 explicitly states that, within this scope, compliance is also mandatory for any potential provider of AI systems.
  • Voluntary use: participation of teachers and students in the use of AI systems is voluntary, after relevant information and training (Articles 2 and 15).
  • Human oversight: AI is used to support learning, not to replace it. Its use and outputs are supervised and assessed by teaching staff (Article 4).
  • No automated grading/profiling: fully automated assessment decisions are not allowed, nor is the creation of individual profiles of students or teachers for such decisions (Article 4).
  • Coordinator: in a school unit where an AI system is used, a teacher is appointed as coordinator of the application's use, in accordance with Article 9.
  • DPIA: Article 13 provides that, before an AI system is used in the educational process, the Ministry of Education, as Data Controller, carries out the required data protection impact assessment (DPIA).
Important distinction: aitools4kids.gr is an independent educational project and is not an official system of, or partnership with, the Greek Ministry of Education. For activities falling under Article 2, the decision states that its provisions also bind potential providers of AI systems. At the same time, the specific procedures for accounts, Greek School Network (ΠΣΔ) authentication, DPAs and administrative roles are described around organised systems of the school process, and no separate procedural route is given for each independent external website. For this reason aitools4kids.gr does not present its classroom mode as “approved” or as proof of compliance: for organised use of live AI in a school activity, the teacher and the school unit must apply the framework and procedures in force that concern them.

What aitools4kids.gr does in classroom mode

Static first.
  • The QR code/deep link for a classroom activity is created without live AI Help by default.
  • The student sees a static, learning-first activity that starts from their own effort and from the school material.
  • Live AI Help is added to the QR code only when the teacher explicitly turns on “Enable live AI Help”.
  • In classroom mode the microphone stays disabled. aitools4kids.gr does not grade students or store student profiles.

What must not be entered

Do not enter students' full names, contact details, photos, voice samples, grades, diagnoses, health data or other personal/sensitive data in prompts or files.

Use at home

The explicit scope of the decision concerns the use of AI in the secondary education process and related programmes/activities. This page does not present the decision as a general ban on families' independent use of AI at home. For every external tool, its own age, account and privacy terms still apply.
