Study material

Do you have a PDF or notes you want to study?

The best tool here is not always a general chatbot. Prefer tools that stay grounded in your own sources or turn existing material into structured revision.

PDFNotesSourcesFlashcards
Quick choice:
  • NotebookLM: when you want answers tied to the sources you supplied.
  • Knowt: when you want to turn material into flashcards and revision modes.
  • Quizlet: when you already have terms or definitions and want structured practice.

NotebookLM — work from your own sources

NotebookLM is useful when you have PDFs, websites or other source material and want to ask questions about that material. Its strength is that the study session begins from the sources you provide rather than an open-ended chat.

See the NotebookLM card →

Useful prompt

“Show me where this chapter explains photosynthesis, then ask me five understanding questions without answering them for me.”

Knowt — flashcards from files

Knowt can create flashcards and revision material from PDFs and notes. Its free plan includes core study modes with limits on AI usage. The service states a minimum age of 13 and requires verifiable parent or guardian consent before AI functionality is used by someone under 18.

Age and account

We do not present it as a direct Primary-school tool. For minors, always check the service’s current consent process.

Open Knowt ↗

Quizlet — when revision is the main goal

If you already have terms, dates, vocabulary or short definitions, Quizlet can be more direct than a chatbot. Younger users receive a more restricted experience, so account and feature availability should be checked.

See the Quizlet card →

What not to do

  • Do not use a summary as a replacement for reading the material.
  • Do not upload personal documents or files containing other people’s information without a reason.
  • Return to the original sources and verify the points you actually need.
