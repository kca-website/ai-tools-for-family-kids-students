What do you want to do with AI?

Do not start with “Which AI is best?”. Start with the task in front of you. A tool for source-based PDF study is not the same as a tool for flashcards, research or visual design.

Free guideTask-firstAge-awareNo ready-made homework
Choose a route:
  • Study a PDF or notes
  • Research with sources
  • Flashcards and revision
  • Presentation, poster or infographic
  • Reading and English practice
  • Creative project with AI

Why start from the task?

General chatbots can do many things, but that does not make them the best choice for every school need. Specialist tools can make sources, revision, reading or visual creation clearer and easier to control.

The site rule

AI Tools for Kids does not teach the lesson instead of the learner. It helps you choose a suitable tool, use it responsibly and identify the next step.

Before opening any tool

  • Check the age requirement and whether a parent or school account is needed.
  • Do not upload personal, school or sensitive information without a clear reason.
  • Verify factual claims and sources instead of assuming AI output is correct.
  • Use AI to organise, understand or practise — not to submit work you do not understand.
